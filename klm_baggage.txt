KLM Royal Dutch Airlines - Baggage Policy

CARRY-ON BAGGAGE (HAND BAGGAGE)
All passengers are allowed 1 small accessory bag (handbag, laptop bag, or small backpack) with max size 40 x 30 x 15 cm.

Economy Class (Standard/Flex): 1 piece of hand baggage, max 55 x 35 x 25 cm, combined weight with small bag max 12 kg
Economy Class (Basic/Light): Only the small accessory bag is free. Hand baggage must be purchased separately.
Premium Comfort: 2 pieces of hand baggage, max 55 x 35 x 25 cm each, combined weight max 12 kg
Business Class: 2 pieces of hand baggage, max 55 x 35 x 25 cm each, combined weight max 18 kg

CHECKED BAGGAGE
Maximum total dimensions (length + width + height) per bag: 158 cm

Economy Standard/Flex: 1 piece, max 23 kg
Economy Basic/Light: 0 pieces included, must be purchased
Premium Comfort: 2 pieces, max 23 kg each
Business Class: 2 pieces, max 32 kg each

EXTRA BAGGAGE FEES
Bags over 32 kg are not accepted as checked baggage and must be shipped as cargo.
Bags exceeding 158 cm total dimensions are considered oversized and incur an extra fee.
Fees vary by route and how far in advance you purchase the allowance.

FLYING BLUE ELITE MEMBERS
Silver, Gold, Platinum and SkyTeam Elite/Elite Plus members receive 1 extra piece of checked baggage in addition to their ticket allowance.

SPECIAL BAGGAGE
Sports equipment, musical instruments, and oversized items require special handling.
Contact KLM in advance for items like bicycles, golf bags, and surfboards.
Musical instruments may be carried in the cabin if they fit in overhead bin or can be purchased a seat.

RESTRICTED ITEMS
Lithium batteries above 160 Wh are not allowed.
Flammable liquids and gases are prohibited.
Liquids in carry-on must be in containers of max 100ml, placed in a transparent resealable bag of max 1 litre.
